uses wing rotation only; never parent these arms under scaled wing bones.
The model builder retains all original vertex data and removes only old arm faces.
Its development inputs are under game/ and work/, as in the earlier builders.

v0.6.1 uses embedded geometry, not JsonUtility or an external runtime arm JSON.

are transformed from the chest with mirrored triangle winding handled explicitly.
